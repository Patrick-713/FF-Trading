THE PLAYBOOK

Game day. Meet market day.

Build a seven-player roster. Your team has a share price, and you can invest demo credits in any team on the exchange.

What moves the price?

Each simulated week, teams score fantasy points. A 120-point score keeps the price steady. Above that, the price rises; below it, the price falls.

Change = (points − 120) / 120 × 25%

Weekly movement is capped at ±12%. Trades transfer shares at the quoted price; trading volume does not move prices in this draft.

All players and results are fictional. Each browser has its own private simulation. This draft has no real money, live NFL feed, or multiplayer league.
